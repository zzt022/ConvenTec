PRODUCT 02

签到助手Check-in Assistant

Check-in & Badge Assistant

展会现场签到、制证、门禁一体化工具。观众扫码自助签到，展商专属码在线填报，操作员一键制证打印，闸机刷证通行，现场数据实时上屏。An all-in-one tool for on-site check-in, badging and access control. Visitors scan QR codes to self check-in, exhibitors fill in their teams online via dedicated codes, operators print badges with one click, turnstiles clear attendees by badge, and on-site data goes live in real time.
